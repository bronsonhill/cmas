# Stanislaw Ulam

Stanislaw Ulam was a mathematician at Los Alamos National Laboratory who, with
[[john-von-neumann]], developed [[cellular-automaton|cellular automata]] in the 1940s,
motivated by an interest in crystal growth.

## Key facts

- Worked alongside von Neumann and Richard Feynman at Los Alamos in the 1940s.
- His interest in crystal growth patterns fed directly into the design of cellular automata
  as a formalism for modelling local, rule-based pattern formation.

## Relevance

Ulam's crystal-growth motivation is the other half (with von Neumann's self-replication
interest) of the CA formalism's dual origin — one researcher interested in growth patterns,
the other in self-reproducing structure, converging on the same modelling framework.
