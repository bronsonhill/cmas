# Craig Reynolds

Craig Reynolds is a computer graphics and animation expert who developed the
[[boids]] flocking model in 1986/87.

## Key facts

- Worked on computer graphics and animation at Electronic Arts, DreamWorks Animation, and
  Sony.
- Coined the term "boid" for the individual agent in his flocking model.
- Boids follows three local rules — separation, cohesion, alignment — sensed within an
  angular, distance-bounded field of view around each boid.

## Relevance

Reynolds's boids is the subject's reference case for [[emergence]]: convincing flock-level
motion generated from three local steering rules, predating the wider adoption of
[[agent-based-model|agent-based modelling]] as a research method.
