# Andrés Muñoz

Subject coordinator and lecturer for COMP90083 Computational Modelling and Simulation at
the University of Melbourne (`munoz.m@unimelb.edu.au`).

## Key facts

- Bachelor's and Master's in electronics engineering; PhD in mechanical engineering;
  subsequently moved into mathematics and computer science through an interest in complex
  systems.
- ~15 years working in mathematics and computer science, ~7–8 of them at the University of
  Melbourne.
- Research interest: [[optimisation]] — measuring, modelling and improving real-world
  systems, and finding solutions rather than only describing behaviour. Applications
  mentioned include transport flow and wind energy.
- Delivers both weekly lectures: Tuesday sessions lean theoretical, Thursday sessions
  demonstrate models and explore parameters and [[tipping-point|tipping points]].
- Tutors for the subject: Anh Pham (`baduyanh@unimelb.edu.au`) and Parsa Babadi Noroozi
  (`p.babadinoroozi@unimelb.edu.au`).

## Relevance to CMAS

Sets the subject's framing: modelling is not just representation but a route to
intervention and policy, which is why [[optimisation]] and decision-making run through the
syllabus alongside [[agent-based-model|ABM]] mechanics.
