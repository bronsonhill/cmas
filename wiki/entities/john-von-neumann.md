# John von Neumann

John von Neumann was a mathematician and physicist at Los Alamos National Laboratory who,
with [[stanislaw-ulam]], developed [[cellular-automaton|cellular automata]] in the 1940s.

## Key facts

- Motivated by an interest in self-replicating machines; his work on self-replication
  inspired [[conways-game-of-life|John Conway's Game of Life]] two decades later.
- Also foundational to game theory, co-authoring its founding formalisation with Oskar
  Morgenstern.
- Worked alongside Richard Feynman and Stanislaw Ulam at Los Alamos in the 1940s.

## Relevance

Von Neumann's work sits at the historical root of two of the subject's core topical areas —
pattern formation (via cellular automata) and, more broadly, systems theory and game
theory, per [[sayama-2015-modeling-and-analysis-of-complex-systems-ch1]]'s map of the
field.
