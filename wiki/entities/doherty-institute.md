# Doherty Institute

The Peter Doherty Institute for Infection and Immunity, a joint venture of the University of
Melbourne and the Royal Melbourne Hospital. It produced the modelling that informed
Australia's COVID-19 policy response.

## Key facts

- Colleagues who have taught COMP90083 worked on the Australian pandemic response with the
  Doherty Institute.
- Some of the models used to develop policy were agent-based models
  ([[agent-based-model]]).
- The modelling addressed questions such as when Australia could reopen its borders and what
  quarantine arrangements were required.

## Relevance to CMAS

The Doherty work is the subject's nearest example of agent-based modelling directly shaping
government policy, and of the conditions under which that modelling happens — decisions made
quickly, under uncertainty, with historical outbreaks such as
[[sars-2003-outbreak]] as the only available precedent. It is also the concrete instance
behind [[covid-19-abm-australia]].
