# Stephen Wolfram

Stephen Wolfram proposed, in 1984, a four-class classification of long-run
[[cellular-automaton]] behaviour — see [[wolfram-classes]].

## Key facts

- 1984 paper argued that many, perhaps all, CA fall into four basic behaviour classes.
- Studied elementary 1D CA rule 30 in detail, noting its resemblance to the pigment pattern
  of the *Conus textile* seashell.
- Identified rule 110 as a Class 4 rule, later proven [[turing-completeness|Turing
  complete]].

## Relevance

Wolfram's classification is the subject's working vocabulary for CA behaviour, giving a
coarse but useful way to say what "complex" means for a single minimal rule table.
