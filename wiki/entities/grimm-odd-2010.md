# Grimm et al. (2010) — The ODD Protocol: A Review and First Update

The 2010 revision of the [[odd-protocol]], led by [[volker-grimm]]. Set as required reading
for Week 2 of COMP90083.

## Key facts

- Revises the original 2006 formulation after several years of use, in response to how
  modellers had actually applied it.
- Published in *Ecological Modelling*.
- Establishes the seven-section structure the subject teaches: Purpose; Entities, state
  variables and scales; Process overview and scheduling; Design concepts; Initialisation;
  Input data; Submodels.
- A further revision followed in 2020.

## Relevance to CMAS

This is the reference students are directed to when writing model descriptions, including
for Assignment 1. The lecture covers the same structure but defers the ten design concepts
to later in the subject, so the paper is the fuller treatment of that section.
