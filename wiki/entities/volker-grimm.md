# Volker Grimm

A German ecologist who formulated the [[odd-protocol]], the standard plain-language template
for describing agent-based and individual-based models.

## Key facts

- Works in ecology; the ODD protocol originated from the individual-based modelling
  tradition in that field.
- Published the first description of the protocol in 2006.
- The protocol has been revised twice since, in 2010 and 2020 — the 2010 revision
  ([[grimm-odd-2010]]) is the subject's assigned reading.

## Relevance to CMAS

The ODD protocol is the framework the subject uses for describing models, and Assignment 1
expects the model description to follow its structure. Grimm's disciplinary background also
explains something about the protocol's design: it was built for a field where modellers
routinely collaborate with people who do not program, which is why it insists on plain
language rather than code.
