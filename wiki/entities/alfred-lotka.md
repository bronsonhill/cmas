# Alfred Lotka

Alfred Lotka was a US mathematician and chemist who, independently of
[[vito-volterra]], formulated the [[lotka-volterra-model|predator-prey model]] that bears
both their names, in 1925–26.

## Key facts

- Independently derived the same predator-prey equations as Volterra, both building on
  Verhulst's logistic growth equation.

## Relevance

Lotka's formulation is the subject's reference continuous-time predator-prey model, and the
bridge case for recasting population-flow ODEs as discrete, [[cellular-automaton|CA]]-
compatible stochastic processes.
