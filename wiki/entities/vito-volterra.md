# Vito Volterra

Vito Volterra was an Italian mathematician and physicist who, independently of
[[alfred-lotka]], formulated the [[lotka-volterra-model|predator-prey model]] that bears
both their names, in 1925–26.

## Key facts

- Independently derived the same predator-prey equations as Lotka, both building on
  Verhulst's logistic growth equation.

## Relevance

Volterra's formulation is the subject's reference continuous-time predator-prey model, and
the bridge case for recasting population-flow ODEs as discrete, [[cellular-automaton|CA]]-
compatible stochastic processes.
