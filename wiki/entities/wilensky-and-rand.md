# Wilensky & Rand — An Introduction to Agent-Based Modeling

The set textbook for COMP90083: *An Introduction to Agent-Based Modeling: Modeling Natural,
Social, and Engineered Complex Systems with NetLogo*, by Uri Wilensky and William Rand
(MIT Press).

## Key facts

- Written around [[netlogo]] — Wilensky is NetLogo's creator — so the book's examples map
  directly onto the subject's tooling.
- Covers [[agent-based-model|ABM]] applied to natural, social and engineered
  [[complex-system|complex systems]].
- Week 1 assigned reading: the first half of Chapter 1 (pp. 21–32), to be read before the
  Thursday lecture.
- Linked from Canvas under additional readings; some readings in this subject are
  mandatory, others suggested for depth.

## Relevance to CMAS

The primary reference text for the subject's foundational material — definitions,
motivation and the NetLogo-based worked examples that the lectures build on.
