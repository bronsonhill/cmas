# Thomas Schelling

American economist and social scientist, author of the segregation model that bears his
name.

## Key facts

- Described in the Week 3a lecture as the North American social scientist behind the
  [[schelling-segregation-model]].
- His question was why segregation persists in populations that describe themselves as open
  and welcoming, and his answer was that mild individual preferences suffice.
- The model predates the availability of computational agent-based modelling in its modern
  form; it is now a standard entry in the [[netlogo]] Models Library.

## Relevance

Schelling's segregation work is the subject's canonical demonstration that population-level
patterns need not reflect individual-level intentions, which is the central claim of
[[emergence]] and a large part of the case for [[agent-based-model]] methods in social
science.

His work on strategic interaction also anticipates the game-theoretic models the subject
covers from Week 6, though the lectures have not made that connection yet.
