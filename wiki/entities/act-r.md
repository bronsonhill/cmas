# ACT-R

Atomic Components of Thought–Rational: a [[cognitive-architecture]] used in basic
cognition research for decades, developed by John Anderson and Christian Lebiere.

## Key facts

- Combines symbolic and sub-symbolic representations of knowledge in one architecture.
- Has been linked to functional regions of the brain, and "successful" for ACT-R means
  closely matching human performance data.
- Focused on relatively **low-level** cognitive phenomena over very short time periods.
  It does not represent beliefs, desires or intentions explicitly — the opposite emphasis
  from [[bdi-architecture|BDI]].
- A model is a collection of declarative facts and rules over environmental and internal
  variables.
- Free, with an active community, annual courses in Europe and the US, and a Java port
  (jACT-R) that has been connected to a mobile robot.

## Relevance

ACT-R's millisecond-scale focus illustrates the mismatch Kennedy warns about: cognitive
architectures were built to answer questions about individual minds, and an ABM asking
about collective outcomes over years of simulated time is paying for resolution it cannot
use. See [[cognitive-architecture]].
